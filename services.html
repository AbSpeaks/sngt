Our Services

Upholding a dynamic balance of spiritual devotion and social responsibility.

Spiritual Temple Poojas

Daily rituals and special prayers performed at the Nashik Gurudeva Mandiram.

Nithya Pooja (Daily Prayers)

Daily morning and evening prayers, deeparadhana, and Gurudeva sthothra pathana to bring peace, mental clarity, and positive energy into the lives of devotees.

Gurudeva Homam

Special spiritual homam conducted on star birthdays (Chathayam star) and other auspicious days to invoke divine blessings for health, success, and prosperity.

Vidyarambham

A sacred ceremony facilitating the initiation of children into the world of letters and education, reflecting the Guru's primary emphasis on learning.

Community Welfare Programs

Selfless support projects carried out by the SNGT Nashik charitable wing.

Scholarship Schemes

Helping bright students from financially backward families secure higher education through annual scholarships, study materials, and college fees support.

Free Medical Camps

Organizing medical camps, blood donation drives, free health consulting, and pharmacy distribution for the local residents of Nashik.

Family Counseling & Legal Aid

Offering free counseling services and basic legal advice to resolve household conflicts and help community members navigate difficult challenges.

Support Our Temple & Initiatives

The Sree Narayana Gurudeva Trust runs entirely on the generous contributions of devotees and well-wishers like you. Your donations support:

  • Construction and maintenance of the temple complex
  • Annadanam (providing free meals to devotees and the poor)
  • Funding education scholarships for underprivileged students
  • Medical aid funds for emergency healthcare cases
