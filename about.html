About Our Trust

Unifying hearts, uplifting souls, and serving humanity in the spirit of Sree Narayana Guru.

Our Mission

To propagate the golden principles of Sree Narayana Guru in Nashik and surrounding regions. We are committed to establishing spiritual sanctuaries, supporting underprivileged education, providing healthcare assistance, and building a united community based on mutual respect, regardless of caste or creed.

Our Vision

A society where humanity is unified as one family ("One Caste, One Religion, One God"). We envision a thriving community empowered by quality education, spiritual enrichment, robust cultural heritage, and selfless charity, leading to the collective upliftment of all.

The Journey of SNGT Nashik

The Sree Narayana Gurudeva Trust, Nashik, was founded by dedicated devotees and followers of Sree Narayana Guru living in Maharashtra. Drawing deep inspiration from the historic **Sree Narayana Dharma Paripalana (SNDP) Yogam** established in Kerala in 1903, the trust represents a major milestone in bringing the Guru's progressive reforms to Central and Western India.

Over the years, the trust has grown from small prayer assemblies to a vibrant organization with its own dedicated temple complex in Nashik. Our temple serves as a focal point where spiritual discourses, traditional rituals, community classes, and massive charitable campaigns are organized. Through our work, we carry forward the Guru's clarion call to "gain freedom through education, strength through organization, and prosperity through industry."

Our Charitable Impact

Putting the Guru’s teachings into practical action through regular community projects.

Gurudeva Educational Aid

Providing financial scholarships, textbooks, uniforms, and learning resources to underprivileged students in Nashik, ensuring education is accessible to all.

Medical Camps & Aid

Conducting quarterly free health check-up camps, eye screenings, distribution of free medicine, and offering financial support for emergency surgeries.

Socio-Marriage Assistance

Facilitating and supporting simple, dowry-free marriage ceremonies for economically weaker families in the community, providing necessary resources.

Sree Narayana Gurudeva Trust

Established to propagate the noble teachings of Sree Narayana Guru in Nashik, Maharashtra. We strive for spiritual harmony, social equality, and charitable empowerment based on the ideals of SNDP Kerala.
